## Errata / modern values (shown on screen where used)
- ¹⁴C half-life: book 5000 years → 5730. The book's «20 периодов → 100 000 лет» is not measurable in practice (2⁻²⁰ ≈ 10⁻⁶); radiocarbon reaches ~50 000 years. Not used.
- Age of the Earth 5,5 → 4,54 млрд лет; Universe 10–12 → 13,8 млрд лет.
- π⁰ lifetime: book ~10⁻¹⁶ s, modern 8,5·10⁻¹⁷ s.
- Second: Cs-133 definition since 1967 (9 192 631 770). Metre: book — wavelength of a spectral line (1960); since 1983 via the speed of light (not shown).
- Uncertainty written as in the book (Δx ≥ h/Δp) for the order of magnitude; ch. 2 used ħ/2.
- Venus radar: closest approach ≈ 4,1·10¹⁰ m → round trip ≈ 276 s.
