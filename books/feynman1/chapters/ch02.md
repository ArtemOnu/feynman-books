## Verified numbers
- Grains: 1 мм sphere of quartz (ρ = 2,65 г/см³): m ≈ 1,4·10⁻⁶ кг ≈ 4·10²⁰ protons → q ≈ 67 Кл; F = kq²/r² ≈ 9·10⁹·4,5·10³/900 ≈ 4,5·10¹⁰ Н ≈ 4,5 млн т — "about three million tons" is the right order. Doubling r: F/4.
- Atom 10⁻⁸ см, nucleus 10⁻¹³ см: ratio 10⁵ (a 5 m room → 0,05 мм speck).
- Light 5·10¹⁴–10¹⁵ Гц (table 2.1 approx). Foucault: period of plane rotation 24 ч / sin φ; Стокгольм φ ≈ 59,3° → ≈ 28 ч; Кито φ ≈ 0,2° → practically none.
- Table 2.2 masses (MeV, modern PDG): e 0,511; μ 105,7; π± 139,6; π⁰ 135,0; K± 493,7; K⁰ 497,6; η 547,9; p 938,3; n 939,6; Λ 1115,7; Σ⁺ 1189,4; Σ⁰ 1192,6; Σ⁻ 1197,4; Ξ⁰ 1314,9; Ξ⁻ 1321,7; Ω⁻ 1672,5.
- Uncertainty: Δx·Δp ≥ ħ/2 (the book prints h/2π-style form without the 1/2; modern form used).

## Errata / modern notes (shown briefly on screen)
- Ω⁻ mass 1680 in the book → 1672 МэВ; Ξ 1311/1319 → 1315/1322; Σ⁻ 1196 → 1197.
- Neutrinos have small non-zero mass (oscillations, 1998); the book's "масса нуль" is the 1960s view.
- Hadrons are made of quarks (Gell-Mann, Zweig 1964); the graviton is still hypothetical.
- "Сила" of interactions: dimensionless coupling, order of magnitude only.
