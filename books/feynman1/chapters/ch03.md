## Verified facts
- Helium discovered in the solar spectrum (eclipse of 1868, Janssen/Lockyer) before Earth (1895). Technetium lines in S-type stars (Merrill 1952) ✓.
- Carbon isotopes: ¹²C 6p+6n, ¹³C 6p+7n, ¹⁴C 6p+8n ✓.
- DNA pairs: A–T, G–C. Code: triplets of bases (1961–1966). 20 amino acids ✓.
- First protein structures: myoglobin (Kendrew 1958–60), haemoglobin (Perutz 1959–60) ✓.

## Errata / modern notes
- Nerve terminal: the scan says acetylcholine is released «5–10 молекул за раз»; a single vesicle holds ~10⁴ molecules. Not used on screen.
- ГДФ/ГТФ in the Russian text: we name the universal energy carrier АТФ (ATP); the Krebs step makes GTP/ATP — on screen «АТФ (ГТФ)».
- The book's DNA letters A, B, C, D with pairs AB, CD: modern A–T, G–C used, with a note.
- «Never understood» mountain building: since the late 1960s — plate tectonics (shown as a note).
- Turbulent pipe flow: still derived empirically / numerically today.
