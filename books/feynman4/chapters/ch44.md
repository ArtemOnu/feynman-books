# Ch. 44 Законы термодинамики — notes
Source: PDF 98–122 (printed 99–123). Beats: heated rubber band lifts a weight · first law Q = ΔU + W · second law (Carnot's postulate: no engine turns heat of one reservoir wholly into work) · Carnot cycle on p–V: isotherm T₁, adiabat, isotherm T₂, adiabat · two reversible engines must have equal efficiency → Q₁/T₁ = Q₂/T₂, η = 1 − T₂/T₁ (thermodynamic temperature) · entropy ΔS = Q/T: zero for reversible cycles, grows otherwise.
Checks: Carnot cycle step; η for 500/300 K; entropy true/false. Practice: Q₂ of an engine; entropy of heat flow; perpetual motion.
