# Ch. 50 Гармоники — notes
Source: PDF 199–216 (printed 200–217). Beats: a musical tone is periodic → harmonics nω · Fourier series: a square wave built from odd sines 4/π(sin x + sin 3x/3 + …) · coefficients aₙ = (2/T)∫f cos nωt dt · consonance: octave 2:1, fifth 3:2 — harmonics coincide · energy theorem ⟨f²⟩ = a₀² + ½Σ(aₙ² + bₙ²) · nonlinear response x + εx² makes 2ω and a constant.
Checks: harmonic of 220 Hz; coinciding harmonics of a fifth. Practice: square wave 3rd harmonic amplitude; nonlinear output.
